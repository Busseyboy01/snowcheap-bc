About SnowCheap BC

SnowCheap BC is an independently run website based in Vancouver, British Columbia. It collects ski and snowboard deals for Vancouver and BC: season pass early-bird deadlines, lift ticket offers, ski swaps, gear sales and student, kid and senior discounts.

How deals are checked

Every listing links to its official source page, and prices and dates are re-checked against that page. Each card shows the date it was last verified. Expired offers move to the archive instead of being deleted. All guides and summaries are original content written for BC skiers.

Independence

The site is not affiliated with any resort or retailer. Some links may be affiliate links (see the privacy policy), but that never decides what is listed. Paid placements are always marked “Sponsored”.
