Privacy policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

What we collect

SnowCheap BC has no accounts, forms or newsletter, and does not collect your name or email unless you email us. If you email us, we use your message only to reply and do not sell or share it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security and operation; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Cookies and affiliate tracking

The site itself sets no cookies. Some outbound links may be affiliate links. When you click one, the retailer or affiliate network (for example AvantLink, Impact, CJ or Amazon) may set cookies on their website to record that you came from this site, so we can earn a commission if you buy something. This costs you nothing extra. Their privacy policies govern those cookies.

Third-party scripts we may add

We may add these services in future:

  • Stay22: a script that turns accommodation links (such as Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com or Vrbo) into affiliate links and may show lodging maps. It may use cookies or similar technology and process your IP address and approximate location to show local results and track bookings.
  • Amazon Associates: as an Amazon Associate we would earn from qualifying purchases. Amazon may set cookies when you follow a link to Amazon.ca.

We will update this page when any such script is added.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use private browsing, without losing access to this site. Blocking third-party cookies may stop affiliate commissions being tracked but will not affect prices.
