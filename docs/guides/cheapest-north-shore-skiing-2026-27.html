Cheapest ways to ski the North Shore in 2026/27

Vancouver's three North Shore hills are Grouse Mountain, Cypress Mountain and Mt Seymour. These are the cheapest options we have verified on their official pages for 2026/27.

Season passes (if you'll ski often)

  • Grouse Mountain 2026/27 Early Bird season pass from $399 (expired), ends Oct 13, 2026. Prices go up October 14. Extra 10% off for families. Unlimited or Weekday passes; perks include 50% off an Ikon Session Pass 3-Day. Source: official page (verified 2026-10-13)
  • Cypress Mountain 2026/27 season passes from $389 (Bronze) / $849 (Gold) (expired), ends Oct 13, 2026. Cypress homepage: 'Season Pass prices go up after Oct 13'. Adult Bronze (weekend nights) $389, Adult Gold unlimited $849. Source: official page (verified 2026-10-13)

A few visits only

  • Mt Seymour 3Ski pass: 3 visits for $139 adult / $119 youth / $79 child. On sale November 3. Save up to 44% vs max-price ticket. Valid Sun 4:30pm-Fri close, blackouts Dec 19-Jan 3 and Feb 13-15. Free shuttle included. Rental add-on $120/$120/$90. Source: official page (verified 2026-10-19)

Kids and youth lessons

  • Grouse snow school: 20% off ski & snowboard programs, ages 3-18. 'For a limited time' - no end date published. Source: official page (verified 2026-10-19)
  • Cypress: 20% off child & youth Snow School multi-day programs. Listed as a limited-time offer; no end date published. Source: official page (verified 2026-10-19)

How to choose

Compare the price of a pass with how many days you'll actually ski, and check its valid times and blackout dates (Seymour's 3Ski, for example, lists blackouts). For live countdowns see Deadlines, and for each resort see Resorts.
