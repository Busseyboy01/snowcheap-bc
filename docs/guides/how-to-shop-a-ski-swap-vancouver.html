How to shop a ski swap in Vancouver

At a ski swap, people drop off used skis, boards and gear to sell, and buyers pay a small entry fee to shop. Here are the swaps we have verified on official pages:

  • Winter Extreme Ski & Board Swap (Canadian Ski Patrol) - Oct 17-18, ends Oct 18, 2026. Sat 9am-5pm, Sun 10am-4pm. Adults $5, kids 6-12 $2, family $10. Sunday 25% off red tags. Gear check-in Fri Oct 16 3-7pm. Source: official page (verified 2026-10-13)

Tips taken from the listings

  • Selling? Check the gear check-in window on the official page. At the Winter Extreme swap it is the day before the sale.
  • Want the lowest price? Some swaps cut prices on the last day. Winter Extreme lists 25% off red tags on Sunday, but the best selection is usually early.
  • Bringing kids? Look for family entry pricing.
  • Before you go, check the official page for the hours, and compare against new-gear prices on our Gear page.
