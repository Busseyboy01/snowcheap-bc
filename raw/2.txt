WHAT Is the Ski and Board Swap? We sell new and used ski and board equipment on consignment at huge discounts from retail - one weekend only! SKIS New and used skis of all shapes and sizes. Most come with bindings, while some are still in the wrapping! SNOW BOARDS New and used boards of all shapes and sizes. Most come with bindings, while some are still in the wrapping. ACCESSORIES You will find ski poles, ski &amp; board helmets, ski &amp; board binding, goggles, sunglasses and other related items. CLOTHING In addition to the vendor stores, in the swap area you will find ski &amp; board jackets, pants, hats, gloves, mitts and more. Nordic Gear We do often have Nordic skis, poles and boots available, but the selection is much smaller than our Alpine inventory. SKI TOURING While the ski touring industry has become extremely popular in BC, we do not get a lot of inventory of touring gear, but you might just get lucky and find something. Can I bring my own gear to sell? We encourage everyone to clean out their garages, and closets and bring their old gear here to sell. See the times listed above, we only take gear the day before the event begins, and pickup is after the event is closed. How do I sell my gear? Click here for instructions on how to sell your gear.
