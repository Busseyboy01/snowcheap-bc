2026-27 Sun Peaks Alpine Pre-Season Passes

The best days on snow have a little breathing room. With a 2026-27 Alpine Season Pass, Sun Peaks is yours for the winter. Explore over 4,400 acres of skiable terrain across three peaks, with plenty of room to ski at your own pace.

Buy before October 31, 2026, to secure pre-season pricing and save up to $400. Pass holders also receive up to $3,500 in perks and savings, along with flexible, interest-free payment plans.

From powder-filled glades to relaxed afternoons in the slopeside village, make the most of winter at Sun Peaks.

Sun Peaks. At your leisure.

Why a Season Pass Is Worth It

A season pass offers the best value for skiers and snowboarders who plan to spend multiple days on the mountain throughout the winter. Instead of planning around lift tickets, you can ski when conditions are great or when it suits your schedule.

Alpine Buddy Passes (Winter)

Alpine Buddy Passes let you bring a friend at 40% off. Each pass holder receives five (5) Alpine buddy passes for use during the 2026-27 season. Valid for full-day alpine lift tickets only. Discount applies to full-price tickets and excludes the tot season pass.

Blackout dates apply: December 26, 2026, to January 3, 2027, and February 12 to 15, 2027.

How to redeem/distribute:

  1. Visit the online store, log in to your account, click My Account, then select Vouchers.
  2. Five (5) unique Buddy Vouchers will be present on this page.
  3. Select the view/print/email button of the voucher you would like to use/distribute. Email the voucher directly to your buddy or print a physical, take a photo of the voucher or send the number to your buddy.
  4. Your buddy will then enter the voucher code via the online store to complete their purchase.
  5. Each Buddy Pass Voucher can only be used once, keep these vouchers secure as once they are used they will be deactivated. Additional vouchers will not be provided under any circumstance.

Season Pass Terms and Conditions - Refund, University and Family

Alpine Season Pass Refund and Termination Option

To be eligible for a refund, this option must be added at the time of purchase. Passes purchased without this option are non-refundable.

Price: $35 individual or $80 family.

For full terms and conditions, and to learn how to request a refund, see the refund and termination policy.

University Student Season Pass Conditions*

  • University students must present proof of full-time enrollment for the fall 2026 and winter 2027 semesters to Sun Peaks Resort Access Audit before the start of the winter season. To qualify as a full-time student, they must be enrolled in a minimum of three classes per semester.
  • Apprenticeships and co-op programs may be eligible for a University Student Season Pass provided they are unpaid positions. Applicants under these circumstances must submit confirmation from their respective institutions verifying their participation in an unpaid apprenticeship or co-op program as part of their full-time education.
  • Residents and individuals in paid practicums are not eligible for a University Student Season Pass. 

Send proof of full-time enrollment to accessaudit@sunpeaksresort.com. University Student Season Passes are not activated until proof of enrollment has been provided and approved by Sun Peaks Resort Access Audit.

Alpine Family Season Pass Conditions

Purchase as a family of four or more and save 10% on your Sun Peaks Alpine Season Passes. The discount applies to the five oldest family members (excluding tots) and all subsequent family members are only $100! 

  • A family is defined as a minimum of four pass holders including at least one adult (maximum of two adults) and dependent children under the age of 19, living in the same principal residence.
  • Discounted family pass rates apply to the five oldest family members (excluding tots). All subsequent family members are $100 or $30 for tots (5 and under).
  • Sun Peaks Resort reserves the right to determine final decisions on all family season pass applications.
  • Single Parent Families that do not meet the above requirements please inquire with Guest Services at 250.578.5474.

2026-27 Sun Peaks Alpine Season Passes FAQs

Q: When is the Pre-season sale for Sun Peaks Alpine Season Passes?

A: The Pre-season sale for 2026–27 Sun Peaks Alpine Season Passes runs from May 1 to October 31, 2026. Adult passes are $1,569 CAD during this period.

Q: What is the current price for a Sun Peaks season pass?

A: Pre-season pricing is currently in effect, with adult Alpine Season Passes priced at $1,569 CAD until October 31, 2026.

Q: When should I buy a Sun Peaks Resort season pass?

A: Season passes are currently available at pre-season pricing until October 31, 2026, offering savings of up to $400 before regular pricing begins.

Q: What are the benefits of a Sun Peaks Resort Season Pass?

A: A Sun Peaks Alpine Season Pass includes unlimited lift access throughout the winter season. Pass holders also receive a complimentary Nordic Season Pass, Buddy Passes for discounted lift tickets, and access to savings on rentals, retail, dining and accommodations. Additional benefits include reciprocal lift ticket savings at partner resorts.

Q: Are payment plans available for Sun Peaks season passes?

A: Yes, interest-free payment plans are available, allowing guests to spread the cost of their season pass over time.

Q: What is a Family Season Pass at Sun Peaks Resort?

A: A Family Season Pass offers discounted pricing when four or more family members living at the same principal residence purchase together. The group must include at least one adult (maximum of two) and dependent children under 19. The five oldest family members (excluding Tots) receive 10% off their Alpine Season Passes. Additional dependents are $100 each, or $30 for Tots (ages 5 and under).

Q: Can I get a refund on a Sun Peaks season pass?

A: Refunds are only available to pass holders who purchase the Refund and Termination Option at the time of purchase and meet eligibility requirements. See the refund and termination policy for full details.
