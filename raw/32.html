PASSHOLDER PERKS: ON MOUNTAIN

You must present your 26/27 Big White Season Pass before purchasing to redeem these offers. Please note 26/27 passholders will not have access to on-mountain perks until the 26/27 winter season begins.


SHARE - 2 Buddy Tickets at 50% Off + 2 Tubing Tickets at 50% Off 
All 2026/27 season passholders (excluding tot passes) receive two 50% off ticket vouchers to give to friends and family and two 50% off tubing vouchers to use themselves or to give to friends and family. Log in to your estore account to view, share and redeem your vouchers. View the redemption instructions here. There are no blackout dates for vouchers. Vouchers are redeemed for date specific products and are non-refundable and non-transferable once redeemed. For more information or assistance email bookdirect@bigwhite.com.


NEW FOR 2026/27 - 2 Group Lesson Vouchers at 40% Off
All 2026/27 season passholders (excluding tot passes) receive two 40% off group lesson vouchers to use for yourself or your friends and family. Black out dates for redemption Dec 23-31, 2026 & Feb 13-15, 2027

FREE Night Skiing
Ski under the bright lights in the largest night skiing area in Western Canada. The Bullet Express, Plaza Quad and TELUS Park Chairs offer night skiing Tuesday thru Saturday when available.

FREE Skating Rink Access
‘Canada’s Highest Outdoor Skating Rink’, it’s the same size as an Olympic Hockey rink! Skating is free for everyone so why not invite your friends and family to join you. Skate, stick and puck rentals are available in the Happy Valley Day Lodge for an additional cost.

FREE Nordic & Snowshoe Trails Access
Get off the beaten track and enjoy Big White’s breathtaking back country with over 25 kilometers (15.5 miles) of Nordic trails, there is plenty to see and explore.

10% Off Food & Beverage (excl. alcohol)
Valid at Alpine Burger Co & Alpine Express at Happy Valley Day Lodge, VISTA at Black Forest Day Lodge, The Moose Lounge and Kettle Valley Steakhouse at Happy Valley Day Lodge, SPUDS at the Ridge Day Lodge, Moonlight Bistro at the Gem Lake Warming Hut, The Woods Restaurant + Bar and Clocktower Coffee Co..

Passholders also save 10% (excl. alcohol) at Fourth Ace Bar and Smokehouse.

Exclusive Passholder Accommodation Deals
Save up to 30% on accommodation with Big White Central Reservations. Season passholder deals and specials available throughout the winter season on our website under Hot Deals.

10% Off Rentals
Passholders will receive a 10% discount on any equipment rental at Big White. As an added bonus, bring your friends or family in and receive a 10% discount on their rental too. This offer applies to all rental equipment including, Regular, High Performance, Nordic Skis, Snowshoes and Skates! A valid seasons pass must be shown at checkout and the discount cannot be combined with any other offer.

10% Off Retail Softgoods & Hardgoods
Only available at Big White owned retail stores. Get up to 10% off regular priced outerwear, apparel, skis, boards, boots, bindings & accessories all season long in Altitude, The Rider, Mountainside, Peaks and Dizzy’s Ski Shop. Our retail stores are fully equipped with everything you need to enjoy our mountain. Some exclusions may apply.  NEW: 10% off regular priced product at Altitude this summer.

15% Off Ski & Snowboard Repairs
Damaged your skis or snowboard or just need a tune up? 15% off repairs, 10% off parts from the Big White Repair Shop located in the basement of the Village Centre Mall. *Valid for personal use only.

10% Off Single Group & Private Lessons
Big White Ski & Board School offers a fantastic range of group & private lessons for both children and adults. Seasons Pass holders can book online, by phone or email or in person to receive the discount. Special programs are excluded from the discount.

15% off Big White Logo Merchandise
Enjoy 15% off any ‘Big White’ product or souvenir so you can represent your favourite mountain in style and share our latest giftware with loved ones. Keep your eyes peeled for retail specials exclusive to season passholders! Some exclusions may apply.  NEW: 15% off 'Big White' product at Altitude this summer.

EXCLUSIVE Kids & Youth Season Long Ski Rentals - SOLD OUT 
Our Season Rental program has sold out for 2026/27. Add your name to the 2026/27 waitlist form should we have any cancellations. 2026/27 Season Rental Waitlist - Fill out form

10% off Spa Services & Retail Products
Spa at Stonebridge. Located at Stonebridge Lodge (building 3), the ultimate alpine getaway experience from highly trained spa therapists. Cannot be combined with any other offers.

Chalets at Big White: Season Passholders - Save 30% on your 2026/2027 winter season accommodations when you book before October 18, 2026 and Save 15% when booked from October 19th and on. Visit Chalets at Big White, to explore their properties. New bookings only. Some blackout dates apply. 

alluraDirect Vacation Rentals:  All 2026/27 Season Passholders can enjoy up to $100 in credit towards any Big White vacation rental listed on alluraDirect.com. Credit amount varies based on when the season pass is purchased: $100 for early bird and pre-season passholders, $50 for regular season passholders

 

The purchase of a seasons pass at Big White Ski Resort grants you lift access during hours of operation. All associated perks with the purchase of a seasons pass are based on availability and subject to change. Winter passholder perks are only available during the winter season, unless otherwise stated.

NEW for 2026/27 Vancouver International Mountain Film Festival: Save 15%
Enjoy an evening of inspiring mountain films while supporting Elevation Outdoors! The Vancouver International Mountain Film Festival returns to the Kelowna Community Theatre on Friday, October 23, 2026. Season Passholders save 15% on tickets with promo code BWPromo15

NEW for 2026/27 Hoodoo Adventures: Save 15% off on KVR Shuttles this fall
Big White Season Passholders receive 15% off Kettle Valley Rail Trail shuttle services with Hoodoo Adventures this September and October. Take advantage of the savings and experience the KVR's spectacular fall scenery. Valid for shuttle bookings only, between September 1 and October 31, 2026. 15% off. Rentals are not included. Book up to 5 people using promo code BIGWHITE15 at checkout. Contact direct for groups of 6 or more to receive your discount.

NEW for 2026/27 Specsavers: Save 30% off lens options
30% off lens options with the purchase of 2 sets of frames from $149 and 20% off contact lenses, (only at Orchard Park Kelowna, near Best Buy) Please present your 2026/27 season pass to receive your discount. *Ask in store for details T&C's apply. 

NEW for 2026/27 Denny's & BAR ONE (Kelowna locations): Save 15% off
Season Passholders receive 15% off your bill (excludes alcohol) at Denny's & BAR ONE (Kelowna locations only). Please present your 2026/27 season pass to receive your discount. 

Twin Anchors Houseboat Vacations: Save up to $1,000
Season Passholders receive $500 off 3 or 4-night Cruisecraft houseboat rentals or $1000 off 7+ night Cruisecraft houseboat rentals. Offer is valid for 2026 sailings. Your Season Pass must be presented at time of booking in order to receive this offer. Name on seasons pass must be the same as reservation holder. Located in Sicamous BC on the Shuswap Lake, visit twinanchors.com to learn more about your rental options.

NEW for 2026/27 Save 20% at Fairmont Hotel Vancouver & a $50 F&B credit per stay
As a valued guest of Big White, enjoy 20% off the best available rate plus receive a $50 Food & Beverage credit per stay when you stay at Vancouver’s iconic “Castle in the City.” Book Online

Lakehouse Kitchens: Save $50 off your first cooking class
+ 15% off subsequent classes and cafe purchases. Something fresh is cooking up this season for Big White passholders. Ignite your senses at Lakehouse Kitchens Cooking School. Come visit our welcoming space, flash your pass and enjoy a complimentary cafe beverage. We will also provide a $50 gift card that can be applied towards your first cooking class experience. All season long, you will receive 15% off subsequent classes and all cafe items. Book online using promo code: BIGWHITE15. Not to be combined with other offers.

 

Anytime Sim Golf: 15% off all single-session bookings
Stay golf-ready all winter with 24/7 access to a fully automated indoor golf facility. Big White passholders receive 15% off all single-session bookings. Simply enter your Big White membership number into your Anytime Sim Golf profile and save.

Over The Top Racks & Accessories: 15% off all products
Present your season's pass at checkout and enjoy 15% off all products, up to a maximum discount of $100 per transaction.

Norelco Cabinets: 20% off retail list pricing
With over 50 years of experience and deep roots in the Big White community - from ski-in/ski-out homes, local businesses to the new admin building - and supporter of the Big white community school - Norelco Cabinets is proud to support the spaces that shape the mountain lifestyle. Our showroom and 140-person team, just down the hill in Kelowna, builds more than cabinetry - we build relationships.  

Join the Norelco family and partner with a local team that understands the Big White lifestyle. Whether you're renovating or building new, Passholders enjoy 20% off* retail list pricing on custom, Canadian-made Norelco Cabinets - thoughtfully crafted to elevate your mountain home. *Offer discount excludes countertops, delivery, and installation.

Neighbourhood Brewing: 15% off your bill
Flash your season pass and enjoy 15% off your bill at Neighbourhood Brewing - Penticton. Whether you're winding down after a day on the mountain or just stopping by, we've got tasty tacos, refreshing brews, and good vibes waiting for you in our taproom and on our dog-friendly patio.

Mission Hill Family Estate: 2 for 1 Tastings
Big White Season Passholders enjoy 2 for 1 bar tastings at Mission Hill Family Estate.

Simplex Sportszone: 15% off All Hourly Rates
Big White Ski Resort Season Passholders receive 15% off all hourly rates in any of our simulators at Simplex Sportszone. Discount is only available when you purchase in person, or call ahead to reserve, please present your seasons pass to receive the discount. Please note, discount CANNOT be applied to online bookings. This discount cannot be combined with any existing offers and is not valid on Statutory holidays.

Sagebrush Tours: 15% off Public and Private Tours
Big White Ski Resort Season Passholders receive 15% off private ski shuttles and both public and private wine tours a operated by Sagebrush Tours. Please book here with Promo code: Pass-Holder Savings

Beyond The Crux Climbing Gym: Save 15% off
Big White Season Passholders receive 15% off drop-in day passes and shoe rentals at Beyond the Crux Climbing Gym.

Savoy Rents: 15% off regular (Base) rental rates
Present your Season Pass, discount applied off the base rental rate and will be applied upon return of the equipment at Savoy Equipment ltd.  It does not apply to additional charges that "may" be incurred such as Delivery/Pick up, Fuel, Cleaning Fees, and/or Damage Waiver, and or Damage to the unit.  Customers will need to satisfy regular rental policies such as providing ID upon request as well as Security Deposit hold on their credit card.

15% off all Drop-in Races at Kelowna Karting
Season Passholders save 15% off all drop-in races at Kelowna Karting, cannot be combined with other offers. Please present your Season Pass card or estore receipt of purchase to receive your discount in person at Kelowna Karting. Located at 200 Campion Street, Kelowna, BC

Save 15% at Lakeside Dining
The Hotel Eldorado offers lakeside dining that showcases European-inspired cuisine made from fresh, local ingredients, paired with award-winning Okanagan wines. Guests can enjoy views of Okanagan Lake from the elegant Lakeside Dining Room, the lively Eldorado Lounge and Whisky Room, or the scenic Boardwalk. Signature offerings include Sunday Brunch, celebrated as the best in the region, along with private dining experiences and unique seasonal events. Present your Season Pass to receive your discount. Offer available November 1, 2026 – March 31, 2027.
Exclusions on special events and happy hour pricing.

Save 15% at Maestro’s
Maestro’s Mediterranean offers a warm lakeside ambiance with fresh, coastal-inspired Mediterranean flavors. Weekly events bring unique flavors, great value, and lively energy: Pizza Wednesdays, Pasta Thursdays, Fondue Fridays, and Live Music Saturdays. Open for dinner Wednesday to Sunday, with brunch and happy hour on weekends, Maestro’s Mediterranean invites guests to enjoy vibrant gatherings at any meal. Present your Season Pass to receive your discount. Offer available November 1, 2026 – March 31, 2027.
Exclusions on special events and happy hour pricing.

Save 15% at Café Quattro
Located inside the Four Points by Sheraton Kelowna Airport, Café Quattro offers a welcoming dining experience featuring fresh, locally inspired cuisine, handcrafted cocktails, and a relaxed atmosphere perfect for travelers and locals alike. Guests can enjoy breakfast, lunch, dinner, and casual evening drinks just minutes from YLW Airport and UBCO. Whether stopping in before a flight, meeting friends, or unwinding after a day on the mountain, Café Quattro delivers approachable dining with Okanagan hospitality. Present your Season Pass to receive your discount.
