Why Buy a Season Pass

More mountain for your money

A season pass is the most cost-effective way to ski and ride Cypress Mountain all winter. Buy early, save big, and enjoy perks that make every visit better.

Buy Early, Save More

Season pass prices increase with each deadline. The earlier you commit, the more you save and more value add you receive.

Season Pass Protection Included

Every season pass includes built-in coverage for injury, illness, pregnancy or death. Optional Season Snow Guarantee is also available to protect your investment if snowfall falls short.

Free Parking All Season

Skip the parking fee every time you visit. All season pass holders receive complimentary parking for the entire winter season, so you can drive up whenever conditions are right.

Exclusive Discounts and Partner Offers

Save 15% at Big Bear Sports and the Downhill Repair Centre. Plus, flash your pass at participating local shops and restaurants for additional savings through our Flash Your Pass program.

Reciprocal Mountain Program

Cypress Mountain is proud to partner with several resorts through the Reciprocal Mountain Program. Cypress Mountain Season Pass holders receive discounted lift rates at participating resorts. Details on Winter 26-27 resort partners available in the Fall.

FAQS & PERKS

Got a question?

Everything you need to know about the Season Passes before you buy.

Winter FAQsPassholder Perks

  • Cypress never uses third-parties vendors to sell season passes or lift tickets. Any season pass or lift ticket purchased from anyone other than Cypress is not valid and will thus not be honoured.
  • Cypress reserves the right to change or cancel programs or alter pricing without notice.
  • All products are non-transferable and non-refundable once purchased.
  • Payment Plan: Season passes purchased online by April 23, 2026 are eligible to split up the cost with Affirm with as low as 0% APR. Subject to eligibility. Terms apply.
  • A $500 FINE will be charged and your season pass will be revoked if you are caught attempting to re-sell tickets of any kind or someone other than yourself attempts to use your Seasons Pass/Sky Card (with or without the passholder's knowledge or consent).
  • ID is required when purchasing/redeeming tickets of any kind.
  • Always visit the Mountain Report page for daily operating hours and condition updates before heading up the mountain.
  • Please visit the Hours of Operation page for full season operating schedule.
  • Prices are in Canadian Dollars.
  • Taxes not included.
  • Age categories for season passes are based on your age as of November 1st, 2026.
  • Limited quantities available.

Easy Change | Season Pass Rollover

If you’re a season passholder and need to defer your pass value to next season due to a medical condition or qualifying hardship, visit our Season Pass Rollover page for eligibility details and to submit your request.
