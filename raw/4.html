3Ski Terms & Conditions

  • Only ONE 3Ski product per person, per season allowed
  • Valid from when Mt Seymour's downhill area is open.
  • Blackout dates: Dec 19, 2026-Jan 3, 2027 & Feb 13-15, 2027
  • Operating hours may be reduced at off-peak times throughout the season, typically a later opening on weekdays of 1.30pm, and an early closure on weekends at 4pm.
  • New pass holders are required to pay a $5 new RFID card fee. Reload an existing eligible RFID card to save the $5 new card fee.
  • All 3 visits are loaded onto the one RFID card. Once you have your card, head straight to the lifts. No reservations required!
  • All uses must be redeemed by the end of the 2026/27 season. Uses not redeemed will be forfeited.
  • Non-refundable, non-transferable and not valid for use during advertised blackout dates. No rain checks - we do not offer refunds in the event of rain, snow, or poor visibility. Guests are responsible for checking on weather conditions before deciding to purchase or redeem a visit.
  • Not valid for Season Pass holder benefits including Reciprocal Mountain deals.
  • Rental equipment add-on includes skis, boots & poles OR snowboard, bindings & boots, plus a helmet
  • Shuttle is included for all 3 uses of the pass
  • If a 3Ski Pass is lost or broken, a $5 replacement fee will be charged. Mt Seymour reserves the right to deny pass reprints.
  • 3Ski pass holders are eligible for 20% off lift tickets, day or night, after their 3 visits are used up.

FAQ's

What are the blackout dates?

  • Holiday Period: Dec 19, 2026-Jan 3, 2027
  • Family Day long weekend: Feb 13-15, 2027
  • Operating hours may be reduced at off-peak times throughout the season, typically a later opening on weekdays of 1.30pm, and an early closure on weekends at 4pm.

What happens when I've used all 3 visits?

Used all three of your 3Ski lift tickets but want to come back for more? No problem!

3Ski pass holders can reload their pass and receive 20% off on additional daily lift tickets, including weekends (subject to availability). Buy as many additional days as you like for the remainder of the 2026/27 Season! Call 604.986.2261 option 6, or visit the Season Pass & Ticket Office on hill.

How many 3Ski passes can I purchase?

In order to share the fun, only ONE 3Ski pass can be purchased per person, per season.

Need a buddy to ski/ride with you? Feeling generous? Good news... you can purchase as many 3Ski passes as you like as gifts for others (as long as your buddy hasn't already had a 3Ski pass this season).

What if I forget or lose my 3Ski pass?

Don't panic! If you arrive on-hill and realize your 3Ski pass missed the memo to attend, come and visit us at any of our Guest Service locations. If you have forgotten, lost or broken your pass, you will be required to pay a $5 + tax replacement card fee.

What if I am not able to use one or more of my visits?

As per the terms & conditions of purchase, 3Ski products are non-refundable and non-transferable once purchased. You must use all 3 visits by the end of the season, as they expire once closed for the 2026/27 season (see our Hours of Operation page for season dates).

We understand that sometimes life happens! If there are extenuating circumstances that mean you absolutely cannot make use of your 3Ski, please complete our claims form HERE and provide evidence of you claim to claims@mtseymour.ca for individual consideration. Please note that we will not except claims that do not have extenuating circumstances behind them and evidence provided.
